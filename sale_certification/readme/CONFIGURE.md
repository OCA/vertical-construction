Access to certifications follows the Sales access groups. Go to
*Settings > Users & Companies > Users* and, in the *Sales* field of each user:

- **User: Own Documents Only** or **User: All Documents**: the user sees the
  *Certifications* menu and can create, confirm, cancel and invoice
  certifications, retention monies included. No invoicing access group is
  needed for that.
- **Administrator**: the user can also delete certifications.

Users without any Sales access group cannot see or open certifications.
